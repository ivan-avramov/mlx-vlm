### 2b. The marker review is complete — keep it that way after each merge

**`.venv/bin/python dev/check_body_divergence.py --sweep`** is the worklist. It ranks
every content-differing definition in the tree by `gone` (upstream lines missing from
our copy of that file). As of `de6f1ee1`: **61 differ, 22 are strict supersets** — pure
fork addition, nothing upstream lost — and **39 have `gone > 0`. All 39 are triaged.**

`gone > 0` is not a defect. It means a fork replacement *or* content a resolution
dropped, and only `git log -S` tells them apart. What makes the sweep worth running is
the other thing it checks: **a `# Fork:` marker is unverified prose, and five have been
false** (`3105b598` × 3, `0670f556`, `de6f1ee1`). A marker claiming the fork ADDED
something that appears in the `gone` list is wrong on its face. See AGENTS.md, "a
marker is unverified prose".
